# BR1 Bootstrap-Refinement Re-test (2026-07-03)

## Why re-test

The original rejection was evaluated on the mask-corrupted system, where
the joint pose+Gaussian optimization plausibly refined toward
person-contaminated geometry ("re-sculpts the early Gaussians and
destabilizes downstream tracking", per the original analysis). All
week-2 re-tests repeat old accept/reject decisions on the fixed system.

## Protocol

h1 (BONN person_tracking), full mode, current code (through `9ed0af8`),
`slam.bootstrap.enabled: true` with protocol-fixed knobs (n_frames=15,
iterations=200). Seeds 42/123/2024/7, one process per run. Baseline:
8.47 ± 1.20 cm (n=8, `results/sweep/exp_langmask.jsonl`).

## Decision

**Rejection upheld, mechanism updated.** Two findings:

1. Under motion-based keyframe selection only ONE keyframe exists by
   frame 15, so BR1 has essentially nothing to refine (0.3 cm deltas).
   The intervention is structurally inert in the current system, not
   merely unhelpful.
2. The original harm is gone: pre-fix BR1 landed at 9.37 against a
   healthier baseline era; post-fix it lands at-baseline. The
   destabilization was most plausibly the optimizer refining toward
   person-contaminated early geometry — another downstream casualty of
   the since-fixed mask bugs.

Keep `slam.bootstrap.enabled: false`. If bootstrap-window refinement is
ever revisited, it must first increase early keyframe density (or
trigger later) to have more than one pose to optimize.
