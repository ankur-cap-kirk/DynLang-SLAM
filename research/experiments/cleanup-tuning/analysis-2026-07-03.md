# Belief/Cleanup Threshold Tuning (2026-07-03)

## Motivation

After the dynamic-mask fixes, `cleanup_contaminated` fires burst
deletions (7-17k Gaussians in one pass) late in BONN runs, sometimes
near ATE spikes in the raw per-frame trace. Question: are the defaults
(`contamination_thresh: 3`, `cleanup_every_n: 20`) causing damage, and
would stricter evidence or smaller/more frequent cleanups help?

## Protocol

h1 (BONN person_tracking), full mode, current code (through `9ed0af8`),
seeds 42/123/2024/7, one process per run. Baseline: 8.47 ± 1.20 cm
(n=8, `results/sweep/exp_langmask.jsonl`).

## Results (ATE cm)

| config | thresh | every_n | seed42 | seed123 | seed2024 | seed7 | mean ± std |
|--------|--------|---------|--------|---------|----------|-------|------------|
| baseline | 3 | 20 | — | — | — | — | 8.47 ± 1.20 (n=8) |
| c1 | 5 | 20 | 7.88 | 11.59 | 8.41 | 11.07 | 9.74 ± 1.60 |
| c2 | 3 | 10 | 8.29 | 7.36 | 6.46 | 10.69 | 8.20 ± 1.61 |
| c3 | 5 | 10 | 8.40 | 9.56 | 11.87 | 8.27 | 9.53 ± 1.45 |

## Decision

**Keep defaults.** No variant significantly beats baseline; the stricter
threshold (c1, c3) trends worse — prompt deletion of contaminated
Gaussians beats accumulating evidence. The burst deletions are benign
(predominantly correct removals of dynamic-object Gaussians).

Paper note: performance is insensitive to cleanup cadence/threshold
within 2x of defaults — a robustness point for the ablation section.

Raw records: `results/sweep/exp_cleanup_tuning.jsonl`.
