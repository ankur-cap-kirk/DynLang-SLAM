# LP1 v1 Evaluation: REJECTED as registered (2026-07-04)

C1 is ~1.3 SE WORSE. Pre-registered rule (accept if >1 SE better): reject.

## What worked

The mechanism functioned as designed: language evidence entered at
extraction keyframes, deposited belief on a compact Gaussian set, and
the reprojection mask carried it to every frame (mask 14.5% -> 19.6%).
The evidence/inference separation held (no runaway; belief-hot counts
stayed object-sized).

## Why it failed anyway

1. **Vocabulary over-fire.** lang_dyn covered 23-35% of extraction
   frames (smoke) — far more than balloon+person. "An object being
   moved or carried" plausibly matches static furniture. The extra ~5%
   average masking removes static texture the tracker needs; on h2 the
   information cost exceeded the contamination benefit.
2. **Unverified premise.** The assumption that the unmasked balloon is
   a major h2 error source was never directly tested. The balloon is
   small and often fast-moving (blurred, low-weight in losses); the
   person — YOLO-handled — may dominate h2's dynamic error, leaving
   little for the prior to win even in principle.

## v2 design directions (not yet registered)

- **Language proposes, geometry confirms**: keep the open vocabulary,
  but require language-flagged segments to pass the existing
  instance-level depth-motion verification before entering the mask —
  static furniture matching a movable concept is then rejected by
  physics. This is the principled fusion, and it keeps the
  open-vocabulary story intact.
- Tighter vocabulary / higher relevancy_thresh as fallback knobs.
- Premise check first: measure how much of h2's residual error is
  actually attributable to balloon pixels (e.g. oracle mask ablation)
  before investing in v2 tuning.
