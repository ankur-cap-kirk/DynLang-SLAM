# A2 Tracking-Retry Re-test (2026-07-03)

## Why re-test

The original A2 rejection was evaluated on a system with three
since-fixed defects (per-pixel depth-verify gutting dynamic masks to
~2.5% coverage, occlusion-blind belief/cleanup, unmasked SSIM). Any
accept/reject decision made against that baseline is suspect: retry
plausibly compensated for — or was drowned out by — mask-corruption
failure modes that no longer exist.

## Protocol

- h1 (BONN person_tracking), full mode, `rerun_d16_all.py` config with
  `slam.tracking.use_tracking_retry: true`; everything else identical to
  the current baseline (commits through `9ed0af8`).
- Seeds 42/123/2024/7, one process per run.
- Baseline for comparison: full mode with masked language loss,
  8.47 ± 1.20 cm (n=8, `results/sweep/exp_langmask.jsonl`).

## Results

| seed | ATE (cm) | time (s) |
|------|----------|----------|
| 42   | 9.62     | 344 |
| 123  | 8.66     | 357 |
| 2024 | 8.25     | 369 |
| 7    | 12.35    | 309 |

Mean 9.72 ± 1.63 (n=4) vs baseline 8.47 ± 1.20 (n=8): ~1.4 SE worse —
not significant, but no improvement, and runtimes barely moved
(retry rarely fired).

## Decision

**Rejection upheld.** With the dynamic-mask and loss fixes in place, the
tracking failures retry was designed to rescue are rare; when it does
fire it can select a wrong hypothesis (seed 7 outlier). Keep
`use_tracking_retry: false` as default.

Raw records: `results/sweep/exp_a2_retry.jsonl`.
